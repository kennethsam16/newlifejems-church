Bible record highlights

Celebrating devotion to God’s Word.

These achievements were shared by New Life JEMS Church and Isaac Daniel Ministries.

Continuous Bible reading · Tamil

78 hours, 18 minutes

According to the ministry, Pastor H. Isaac Daniel became the first person to read and complete the entire Holy Bible in Tamil continuously, in a record time of 78 hours and 18 minutes—from 6:30 a.m. on March 15 to 3:00 p.m. on March 18. The achievement was honored by the Future Kalam Book of Records and the Universal Book of Records.

Group New Testament writing · Tamil & English

195 people · 3 hours

On February 25, 2024, 195 people hand-wrote the New Testament in Tamil and English between 11:15 a.m. and 2:15 p.m. The manuscript contains 643 pages and 15,914 verses.

Bible artifacts on display

2,000 metres · 97 kg

The ministry has displayed a long Bible project reported to measure 2,000 metres and weigh 97 kg, alongside a miniature Bible display.

Record descriptions are presented as shared by the church ministry.
